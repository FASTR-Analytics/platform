# Antenatal and Penta Vaccination Coverage in Somalia
:::

:::contents{title="Contents" depth=2}

# Introduction
This report examines the quality of routine HMIS reporting that underpins antenatal care and Penta immunisation monitoring in Somalia. It covers four indicators — ANC1, ANC4, Penta1 and Penta3 — across 957 reporting facilities in 6 regions and 18 districts, for the period January 2022 to April 2025.

The report does not present coverage volumes. Instead it asks a prior question: how far can the coverage figures built from this data be trusted? It does so through four lenses — completeness, outliers, internal consistency, and a composite facility-level score.

:::callout{kind=note title="Reading the 2025 figures"}
2025 rows cover January–April only. Treat every 2025 comparison as year-to-date, not a full-year result.
:::

# Executive Summary
Somalia's routine reporting on antenatal care and Penta immunisation has grown markedly more reliable since 2022. Reporting completeness for ANC1, ANC4, Penta1 and Penta3 rose from 83% to a peak above 91% in 2024, statistical outliers in these four indicators stay within the good range in every region, and the logical checks that ANC and Penta records should pass (first visit at or above fourth, first dose at or above third) now hold in 95–100% of periods everywhere.

The remaining gap sits at the facility level. The composite score that combines completeness, outliers and consistency still marks under three-quarters of facilities as adequate, and one region — Region 002 — has shown almost no improvement since 2022. A separate and much larger consistency problem surrounds delivery data, which this report flags but does not investigate in depth.
:::steps
Prioritise data-quality support in Region 002, the only region whose facility-level score has not meaningfully improved since 2022.

Sustain the completeness gains of the past three years as new facilities join the reporting system, and watch the early-2025 easing closely.

Investigate the delivery indicator pair separately: its 0–53% regional consistency pass rates sit far outside the pattern seen in ANC and Penta data and point to a distinct reporting problem.
:::
:::

# Assessing ANC and Penta Reporting Quality
How complete, accurate and internally consistent is Somalia's routine reporting on antenatal care and Penta immunisation, and where does it still fall short?
:::

# Reporting Completeness Over Time
The monthly data behind this trend shows a system that found its footing in 2022, held broadly steady through 2023, peaked in late 2024, and has eased every month so far in 2025.

![Reporting completeness for ANC and Penta indicators, 2022–2025](figure:5ccb76d1-fdb9-4370-a063-d0ff4e33811d)

Penta1 and Penta3 completeness started at just 5.7% and 0.2% in January 2022 — a start-up artefact as these two indicators came online — before jumping to 69.6% and 71.0% the following month and converging with ANC1 and ANC4 by mid-2022. From April 2022 onward, all four indicators moved within a comparatively narrow band through 2023, with ANC1 generally highest (87–95%) and ANC4 lowest (84–90%). Completeness then climbed to its best levels late in 2024, with ANC1 and Penta1 both briefly above 97% and 94% in November–December. Every indicator has fallen in each of the first four months of 2025 reported so far, from those late-2024 highs down to 86–90% by April — the clearest sustained monthly decline in the whole series, though still too short a run to say whether it is seasonal or a genuine reversal.

# Reporting Completeness by Region
Completeness is more even across regions than across indicators, but a modest gap remains.

![Reporting completeness by region, ANC and Penta indicators](figure:ea58e2b6-fd74-43af-86ad-73451f9b715f)

Averaged across the four indicators over the full 2022–2025 period, Region 006 reports most completely at 92.0%, well ahead of every other region. Regions 001 and 003 follow in the high 87–88% range, and Regions 002 and 005 sit just behind at 87.8% and 86.1%. Region 004 trails the group at 85.9%, driven mainly by a lower ANC4 completeness rate (83.5%) than any other region records for any of the four indicators. The gap between the strongest and weakest region is about six percentage points — a real but moderate spread, and far narrower than the differences this report finds later in facility-level composite quality scores, where the best and worst regions differ by more than thirty points.

# Outlier Rates in ANC and Penta Reporting
Outlier rates — the share of monthly values flagged as implausible against a facility's own reporting history — have stayed low everywhere over the full 2022–2025 period, though a regional pattern sits beneath that aggregate. Every region keeps all four indicators within the good range defined for this check (under 5%), but the specific indicator driving each region's rate differs.

![Outlier rates for ANC and Penta indicators, 2022–2025](figure:0c0eba61-668a-4fa0-b618-a683d71cd4ae)

Region 005 has the highest average outlier rate across the four indicators, at 1.9%, driven mainly by ANC1 (3.0%) and Penta1 (2.0%). Region 002 follows closely on ANC1 alone (2.6%), despite otherwise clean data on the other three indicators. Region 006 has the lowest overall average (1.2%) and the cleanest ANC1 and ANC4 records of any region (0.6% and 0.2%), but it is not uniformly best: its Penta3 outlier rate (2.2%) and Penta1 rate (1.9%) are both among the highest of the six regions. This split — strong on ANC, comparatively weaker on Penta — suggests that completeness and cleanliness do not always move together within the same region, and that the two deserve separate monitoring rather than a single combined judgement.

# Internal Consistency of ANC and Penta Data
A sub-national area passes a consistency check when its reported values respect an expected relationship — for instance, that first antenatal visits should never fall below fourth visits, or first Penta doses below third doses. Across the full 2022–2025 period and all six regions, ANC and Penta data pass these checks far more often than delivery data does, and the gap between them is not a narrow one.

![Internal consistency: ANC, Penta and delivery indicator pairs](figure:541caa42-f867-4cc3-a070-a6bcb8868755)

:::columns{cols=2}
:::col{tone=accent}
**ANC and Penta hold together everywhere.** The ANC1-ANC4 check passes in 97.5–100% of periods in every single region, and the Penta1-Penta3 check in 94.9–100%. No region falls outside the good range on either pair.
:::
:::col{tone=warm}
**Delivery data does not.** The institutional-delivery check fails completely — 0% — in three of six regions (001, 002 and 005), and passes only 26–53% of the time in the other three. No region comes close to the ANC or Penta pass rates.
:::
:::

This report's scope is ANC and Penta reporting, so the delivery inconsistency is not investigated further here, but its scale is large enough that it should not be set aside: a check that fails completely in half the country's regions points to a distinct, structural reporting problem that merits its own review.

# Facility-Level Data Quality by Region
The composite score behind these checks — the share of facilities meeting a combined threshold for completeness, outliers and consistency — tells a less even story than completeness alone. It folds all three checks into one pass/fail judgement per facility, and its regional path is not a straight climb.

![Facilities meeting the data-quality threshold, by region](figure:ae62ae90-5caf-48ea-aecc-3f1a029a2c2a)

Region 001 rose steadily from 57.9% in 2022 to 84.8% in 2025 YTD, and Region 006 moved from 72.6% to 84.1% with a small dip in 2024 (to 80.1%) along the way. Region 004 followed a similar shape: up sharply to 73.1% in 2023, back to 68.8% in 2024, then up again to 84.1% in early 2025. Region 005 and Region 003 both show the same dip-and-partial-recovery pattern around 2024 rather than a steady trend, and neither has reached 70% as of 2025 YTD. Region 002 is the clear outlier: its score has moved within a narrow 52–60% band across all four years and sat at just 52.2% in early 2025, no higher than its 2022 starting point.

:::callout{kind=info title="Reading the composite score"}
This threshold combines completeness, outliers and consistency into one pass/fail line per facility. A region can look reasonably strong on completeness and outliers, as Region 002 does, and still post a weak composite score if enough of its facilities fall short on any single check — which is why this section's regional ranking differs from the completeness and outlier rankings shown earlier.
:::

## Key Findings at a Glance
:::slot{name=items}
**Reporting completeness improved.** Combined ANC/Penta completeness rose from 83% in 2022 to a 2024 peak above 91%, easing to 90% in 2025 YTD.

**Outliers stayed within the good range everywhere.** Every region keeps all four ANC/Penta indicators under 3% outliers, well inside the standard threshold.

**ANC and Penta logic checks hold; delivery's does not.** ANC and Penta pairs pass 95–100% of periods in every region; delivery passes just 0–53%.

**[Region 002 is not improving.]{.danger}** Its composite facility-quality score has stayed within a narrow 52–60% band across all four years.

**Three of six regions crossed the 70% quality threshold.** Regions 001, 004 and 006 all reached roughly 84% by 2025 YTD, up sharply from 2022.

**The 2025 figures are year-to-date.** They cover January–April only; every 2025 comparison here is partial-year, not final.
:::

# Conclusion and Recommendations
Three years of data tell a consistent story: Somalia's HMIS reporting on ANC and Penta services has become substantially more complete, with outliers held within a good range nationwide, and with ANC and Penta records passing their internal logic checks almost everywhere. What has not caught up is the share of facilities meeting the full combined standard, and that gap is concentrated rather than spread evenly.

The priority is not more indicators but more consistent facility-level support where it is missing. Region 002's stagnation, in particular, sits apart from a national trend that is otherwise positive: every other region has gained at least ten points on the composite score since 2022, while Region 002 has gained almost none. The delivery-data inconsistency flagged in this report, though outside its scope, is large enough to warrant its own follow-up, since a check that fails completely in half the country's regions is unlikely to reflect clinical reality alone.
:::steps
Target Region 002 for facility-level data-quality support, since its composite score has not moved in four years while every other region's has.

Maintain current completeness practices nationally, and watch closely whether the early-2025 easing continues once full-year data is available.

Commission a focused review of institutional-delivery data consistency, separate from this ANC/Penta assessment, starting with the three regions at 0%.

Re-run this assessment once full-year 2025 data is available, to confirm whether the early-2025 dip in completeness is seasonal or a genuine reversal.
:::

## About the Data
:::slot{name=panels}
:::card{title="Sources" icon=database}
Monthly facility reports drawn from Somalia's routine Health Management Information System, covering 957 facilities across 6 regions and 18 districts. The data-quality metrics in this report — completeness, outliers, consistency and the composite facility score — come from the M1 Data Quality Assessment module run against the HMIS dataset, version 13. Only four indicators are in scope: ANC1, ANC4, Penta1 and Penta3; the delivery indicator pair appears only as a consistency comparison.
:::
:::card{title="Coverage" icon=map}
January 2022 to April 2025, covering every facility that reported ANC1, ANC4, Penta1 or Penta3 in that window. The 2025 figures are a partial year through April and are presented alongside full years for 2022 through 2024; they should not be read as full-year results. Regional tables use the full period unless stated otherwise, since monthly regional volumes are too thin to support a reliable last-twelve-months snapshot alone.
:::
:::card{title="Methods" icon=chart}
Completeness is the share of expected facility-periods that reported. Outliers are flagged using the median absolute deviation method at a default threshold, then compared against the 0–5% good, 5–10% acceptable, and above 10% investigate bands. Consistency checks whether related indicator pairs — ANC1 against ANC4, Penta1 against Penta3, and institutional delivery — hold their expected ordering in each area-period. The composite facility score combines all three into a single pass or fail threshold per facility.
:::
:::card{title="Limitations" icon=alert}
This report assesses data quality, not service coverage: it does not estimate how many women or children actually received antenatal care or Penta immunisation. Thresholds for the composite score follow standard FASTR ranges and may need local adjustment to reflect Somalia's own reporting context. No boundary map data was available for this results package, so every geographic result in this report is shown in a table rather than a map.
:::
